Manual APworld for use with the Archipelago randomizer.

Unlock Equipment, Equipment Upgrades, and different haunted Locations by completing Objectives in different locations. Complete the Primary Objective in every location to reach your goal.

NOTE:
It is heavily recommended to play this on a level where most equipment is unlocked (at the very least have some tier III equipment). Technically the minimum level is 17, because that is when the player has every map unlocked (or if the player has Prestiged once before), but the recommended level is 80 (when all gear is accessible).
You start unlocking Tier III equipment past lv 40 so if you have a time crunch then that's the recommended minimum level, especially since you will be earning money and EXP as you play so you'll be levelling up during the Archipelago.

Items:
-Additional Equipment (you start with only the starter equipment that the game begins you with)
-Upgrades (Equipment starts at Tier I and these progressively increase the tier of a piece of equipment by 1)
-Haunted Locations
-(Optionally) Cursed Possessions

Starting Items:
-1 Random Small Map
-The Vanilla Starter Equipment

Checks:
On each map, there are checks for
-Completing the Primary Objective (correctly identifying the ghost)
-Completing 1-3 Optional Objectives
-Finding the Bone (on larger maps, Surviving a Hunt can count for this check)
-Getting a Ghost Photo
-Getting an Audio Recording of the Ghost's Voice
-Using the Cursed Possession
-Earning a Certain Media Reward at the end of an investigation (the value ranges from $50-$60-$75, with smaller maps requiring more $ and large maps requiring the least $)
-Getting a Perfect Investigation (Some big maps don't have this check, because finding the bone can be unreasonably difficult on big maps)
There are also optional modes:
-Media Maestro adds many checks based on different forms of media you can capture.
-Cursed Quests add many checks based on completing tasks with Cursed Possessions
-Easter Eggs add a handful of checks, ranging from completing the Ferryman of the Drowned/Ghost in the Machine, to stealing random objects from the maps.

Goal:
-Complete the Primary Objective on every map

It is up to the player if checks require the player to survive (unless specified).
It is also up to the player if the ghost needs to chase the player for it to count as 'Surviving a Hunt'.
The recommended gamemode to play on is Professional or Nightmare as a baseline, but this is not enforced, especially with Custom Gamemode shenanigans.
For 'Using the Cursed Object' the player can choose if this means doing one action (ie. Pulling one Tarot Card, Making one Monkey's Paw Wish, Pulling one pin in the Voodoo Doll), or if it means fully consuming the Cursed Possession (ie. Pulling the Full Tarot Card Deck, Breaking the Ouija Board/Cursed Mirror, or Pulling the Heart Pin of the Voodoo Doll). Either Interpretation works.

Tips:
This will be a major test of your Phasmophobia skills and knowledge. Knowing each ghost type and what they do (including their unique behavior, evidence types, hunt speeds, and hunting sanity) will be extremely helpful in completing checks when you have limited resources.
The in-game journal's information can be a mixed bag, so my best recommendation is Zero Network's Unofficial Phasmophobia Cheat Sheet:
https://zero-network.net/phasmo-cheat-sheet/
It's my personal favorite guide for learning specific information about ghost behavior and the different maps. However there are many other places online that you can find information about the game.

Good luck out there, and Happy Hunting!
